About

Hello! I'm David, nice to meet you!

I am a UX/UI designer who recently graduated from Purdue University with a bachelor's degree in UX Design. I am bringing with me three years of experience working in cross-functional teams with real clients through multiple university projects.

As a designer, I strive to do more than make products look good. I ensure they make sense to people. I am currently adapting AI and using it to quickly build solutions that I can deploy myself, which you'll find in this portfolio.
