03 · the split

Foundation stays. Theme changes.

Every decision lives in one of two layers. A new product only picks a theme; the foundation comes along for free.

whatgame daycity test
Brand colorScoreboard orangeHarbor teal
TypefacesBebas Neue + InterPublic Sans
Corners16px controls, 24px cards4px controls, 8px cards
MotionPress, slide, confettiNone
Voice"You're in! Go Foghorns!""Saved. You can change this later."

One brand color goes in; Game Day's contrast engine turns it into four accessible versions for text, edges, fills and the text on top of them, in both light and dark.

04 · the rules

Some things never change.

Contrast

4.5:1 for text, 3:1 for controls, icons and the focus ring, in every theme.

Big enough to tap

Buttons and fields at least 48px tall; links and menu options at least 44px.

Never color alone

Chips get a check mark, errors get an icon and words.
